RePLAI4U: Responsible Personalizing the Lifecycle of AI for Users

Personalized AI is here. Are we ready? Bridging ML, HCI, ethics, and law to chart a responsible path for user-adaptive AI.

Submit Paper Invited Speakers View Schedule

About the Workshop

Bringing together researchers and practitioners from machine learning, HCI, ethics, law, and industry to advance responsible personalization across the AI lifecycle.

Modern AI models can adapt their outputs to individual users' preferences, values, and stylistic choices — moving far beyond earlier recommender systems. While personalization improves user experience and supports accessibility, it also introduces risks: misaligned systems may reinforce stereotypes, limit exposure to diverse perspectives, and raise concerns about privacy, consent, and legal compliance. This workshop addresses these challenges head-on.

Operationalizing Responsible Personalization

Frameworks, audit methods, and metrics for quantifying responsible personalization practices aligned with ethical AI principles.

Data Curation

Responsible data practices for personalized model training, including regulatory compliance and data-efficient continual adaptation.

Methods

Privacy-preserving training, meaningful consent and control, and personalization methods that uphold user autonomy.

Evaluation & Benchmarking

Personalized benchmarks that account for legitimate individual differences and frameworks for evaluating systems against global standards.

Societal Impacts & Applications

Societal-level impacts including sycophancy, safety risks, polarization, and the tension between personalization and pluralism.

Interdisciplinary Approaches

Technical methods, legal and policy analysis, critical STS perspectives, HCI research, and qualitative user studies working in synergy.
